# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Spotify listeners who play Rocksmith and want to move between the music they
already enjoy and community-created Rocksmith content.

## Product Purpose

Ignite for Spotify helps people find Rocksmith CDLC that matches their Spotify
playlists and turn CDLC discoveries into Spotify playlists. Success means users
can move in either direction without manually searching two separate catalogs.

## Positioning

Ignite bridges a user's Spotify library with the CustomsForge Ignition catalog:
playlist matching starts from Spotify, while discovery and export start from
Rocksmith CDLC.

## Operating Context

Users connect a Spotify account, select or paste a playlist, review matched
Rocksmith CDLC, and follow download links. They can also search the CDLC catalog
and create a Spotify playlist from selected songs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Spotify authorization is required for account, playlist, matching, and export
  workflows.
- Match My Music preserves the latest completed result for each user playlist
  until it is refreshed or deleted.
- CDLC is community-created content hosted outside Ignite; Ignite provides the
  bridge and search experience rather than hosting downloads.
- Privacy and support information must remain public before Spotify connection.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Ignite for Spotify name, direct and practical voice, established
dark interface, Spotify-green accent, and current matching and discovery
workflows.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the working Spotify authorization, playlist matching,
saved-result, CDLC discovery, export, analytics, diagnostics, and deployment
implementations. No testimonials, customer claims, or commercial guarantees
are available and none should be invented.

## Product Principles

- Make the Spotify-to-Rocksmith bridge obvious.
- Keep user control visible before connecting or deleting data.
- Prefer factual status and privacy language over promotional claims.
- Preserve completed user work when ordinary operations fail.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Core navigation, account actions, matching controls, dialogs, and public
information must remain keyboard-accessible, responsive, and understandable
without relying on color alone.
